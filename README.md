# Skill Vault

A growing library of skills, workflows, frameworks, ideas, and practical knowledge across **Marketing, Product Management, and Data Science & Analytics**. Skill Vault is my evolving knowledge repository where I document things I learn, explore, experiment with, and find useful.

It brings together practical resources, reusable skills, interesting concepts, and documented learnings across three professional domains. The goal is to build a structured collection of knowledge that I can revisit, improve, apply, and share with others.

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Marketing

A collection of marketing knowledge, strategies, frameworks, workflows, experiments, and practical skills. This covers everything from understanding customers and markets to creating campaigns, building brands, driving growth, and measuring performance.

### 2. Product Management

An evolving collection of product management concepts, methodologies, frameworks, tools, and practical learnings. This includes exploring how products are researched, conceptualized, designed, built, launched, and improved.

### 3. Data Science & Analytics

A growing collection of concepts, techniques, tools, and practical experiments related to working with data. This includes exploring how data can be collected, analyzed, interpreted, visualized, and used to make better decisions.

These pillars represent broad areas of exploration, not fixed boundaries. The repository will evolve as I discover new topics, learn new skills, and explore different areas within and beyond these domains.
